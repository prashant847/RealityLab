# Scoring rubric

_Generated by `backend/tools/gen_scoring_doc.py` from `app/scoring/rubric.py`. Do not edit by hand._

Rubric version 1. **No language model is involved in scoring.** Every number comes from recorded state, recorded events, or the same exhaustive planner that detects dead ends.

## Categories (each scored 0–100)

| Category | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Staff safety & risk management | 20% | Whether people were kept safe, and whether safety procedure was followed. |
| Cargo records & evidence reliability | 15% | How trustworthy your cargo numbers and your evidence trail were. |
| Financial discipline | 10% | Keeping effort in reserve and meeting obligations. |
| Recovery speed | 10% | How far systems were actually restored, and how much time was left. |
| Delivery prioritization | 25% | How much of what was needed actually moved, urgent customers first. |
| Stakeholder coordination | 8% | Whether your team stayed with you through the decisions. |
| Communication quality | 7% | How the story was handled once it leaked. |
| Adaptability to new information | 5% | Whether decisions stayed sound after things went wrong. |

### Formulas

- **Staff safety & risk management** (20%): `staff_safety − 15 × [operations continued without a qualified safety check]`
- **Cargo records & evidence reliability** (15%): `0.45 × evidence_quality + 0.40 × verified_share + 0.15 × shipment_reliability, where verified_share = min(100, 100 × verified_units ÷ (hospital_need + contract_need)) and shipment_reliability = max(0, 100 − 5 × phantom_units_shipped)`
- **Financial discipline** (10%): `50 + 50 × min(1, budget_left ÷ 3 points) − 25 × [invoice defaulted] − 25 × [penalty unpaid] − 15 × [most of the effort committed early]`
- **Recovery speed** (10%): `0.50 × system_integrity + 0.30 × recovery_progress + 0.20 × min(100, 100 × time_slack_left ÷ 120 min)`
- **Delivery prioritization** (25%): `100 × (0.70 × min(1, hospital_units_shipped ÷ hospital_need) + 0.30 × min(1, contract_units_shipped ÷ contract_need)) − 10 × [urgent customers deprioritized]; 0 if the reopening deadline was missed`
- **Stakeholder coordination** (8%): `average of the five stakeholders' trust in you`
- **Communication quality** (7%): `public_trust + 8 × [factual statement or coordinated briefing] − 15 × [silence] − 10 × [leads disagreed publicly]`
- **Adaptability to new information** (5%): `100 × (decisions rated sound ÷ decisions) counted from the first setback onward (all decisions if there was no setback)`

## Overall score

`overall = Σ weight × category score`, then capped by the ending:

- safety failure: at most **40**
- missed dispatch: at most **35**
- critical shortfall: at most **60**

Bands: **Exemplary** ≥ 90, **Strong** ≥ 75, **Adequate** ≥ 60, **Weak** ≥ 40, **Poor** below 40.

## Judging decisions fairly

Hindsight is not fair, so a decision is never judged by its outcome alone. Each decision is replayed from the moment it was made in 6 *plausible worlds* that differ only in what the player could not know: the future chance events, and the true (hidden) stock level (±30 units). The value of an option is the best ending still reachable afterwards (the same tiers the dead-end detector uses: 3 hospitals fully served, 2 partial, 1 shortfall, 0 missed or safety failure).

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ev` | mean tier over the plausible worlds |
| `p_objective` | share of plausible worlds in which hospitals could still be fully served |
| `regret_ev` | best option's `ev` − chosen option's `ev` |
| rating | **sound** if regret ≤ 0.34; **questionable** if ≤ 1.0; **poor** above |
| `warned_by` | advisors who opposed the chosen option at the time, with reasons based on information the player could see |
| `impact` | change in the readiness score (same formulas, delivery projected from verified stock and capacity) between the decision and the next decision, including events that fired in between |
| unlucky | rated sound, nobody warned against it, and a chance event (probability < 1) that the decision exposed you to then happened. Bad luck is attributed to the **latest decision that made the event possible**, or, for events nothing could have prevented, to the decision just before it fired. Also used when a different option happened to do better in this run's actual world |
| lucky | rated questionable or poor, yet nothing better was reachable in this run's actual world |

A *sound* decision can still be warned against and cost points; the report then says so ("Feasible, but Arjun and Nisha warned against it…") rather than calling it good or bad outright.

## Losses, opportunities, alternatives

- **Avoidable loss**: a harmful event traced to the **latest** decision at which a comparably sound alternative (within the sound band) would have made the event impossible. Earlier chances to prevent it are listed too. Chance-dependent events show their probability.
- **Unavoidable / chance**: no earlier decision could have prevented it.
- **Missed opportunity**: a rewarding event that never fired and what earns it, plus units short of the hospitals' and contract customer's need and which limit (verified stock or transport capacity) bound it.
- **Recommended alternative**: for questionable or poor decisions, the more feasible option; for sound-but-warned decisions that cost points, the option the warning advisor recommended. Always with the immediate differences.
- **Adaptability** is the share of decisions rated sound from the first setback onward.

## What is revealed after the run

Only a finished run's report includes the true stock level and how much of it was never verified.
