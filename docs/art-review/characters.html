What would actually work (decisions for you)

  1. Commission or find an artist for six consistent characters (at minimum Arjun and the director, 5 to 8 expressions each, plus a few poses). The briefs already exist in public/assets/art/manifest.json. The pipeline needs only transparent PNGs at the stated sizes. This is the only route to the target quality.
  2. Ask the Little_Fiddle artist for permission and a restyle: the cast is the right ethnicity and was made without generative AI, but the style is simple and the licence is unstated.
  3. Accept AI-generated third-party art (for example the AssetSmithy set) as a deliberate exception. I recommend against it: it conflicts with your rules, the redistribution clause is risky for a public web app, and the characters do not match.
  4. Change how the scene is shown without changing the story: depict the characters through hands, screens, silhouettes and over-the-shoulder views built from openly licensed photographs, and keep the portraits as placeholders until real art exists. This needs your approval because it alters the panels.
